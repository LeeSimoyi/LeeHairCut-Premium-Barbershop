Who We Are

Crafting Style. Delivering Confidence.

LeeHairCut was born in 2014 from a simple belief — every man deserves to look and feel his absolute best. Founded in Mutare, Zimbabwe, we set out to create a grooming space that blends precision barbering with a luxury experience unlike anything the city had seen.

Over the past decade, we have served over 5,000 happy clients and built a reputation as Mutare's most trusted premium barber shop. Our team of skilled barbers continuously trains in the latest techniques to ensure every visit exceeds expectations.

From classic cuts to full luxury packages, every service is delivered with care, precision and a genuine commitment to your confidence and style.
