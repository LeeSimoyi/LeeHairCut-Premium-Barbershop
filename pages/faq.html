Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LeeHairCut services, bookings and policies.

Booking

Walk-ins are always welcome subject to barber availability. However, we strongly recommend booking in advance — especially for weekends — to guarantee your preferred time slot and barber choice.

You can book through our online booking page, send a WhatsApp message to +263 78088 6795, call us directly, or simply walk in during business hours. Online booking is available 24 hours a day.

We ask that you cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before your appointment time. This allows us to offer your slot to another client. Late cancellations and no-shows may result in a cancellation fee.

Absolutely. You can request any of our barbers when booking online or over the phone. Subject to their availability on your chosen date and time, we will do our best to accommodate your preference.

Service durations vary: Classic Haircut 30 min, Beard Styling 20 min, Hair Spa 45 min, Face Clean-Up 25 min, Standard Package 50 min, Premium Package 90 min, Luxury Package 120 min.

Services

We offer Classic Haircuts, Beard Styling, Hair Spa treatments, Face Clean-Up, Hot Towel Shaves, Scalp Massages, Hair Colour, Kids Haircuts, Eyebrow Shaping and various combo packages. View our full services page for details.

Yes, we offer a gentle Kids Haircut service for children aged 3 to 12 years at $2.00. Our barbers are patient and experienced with young clients to ensure a comfortable, stress-free experience.

We exclusively use premium, internationally certified grooming products that are safe for all hair and skin types. If you have specific allergies or sensitivities, please inform your barber before your service begins.

Every tool — clippers, scissors, combs, razors — is thoroughly cleaned and sterilised using professional barbicide solution after every single client. We maintain the highest hygiene standards at all times.

Pricing

We accept Zimbabwe Gold (ZiG), USD cash, EcoCash, OneMoney, and major debit and credit cards. All prices displayed on our website are in USD with ZiG equivalent available at the current official rate.

We offer a 20% first-visit discount on your Classic Haircut — simply mention it when booking. Student discounts are available with a valid student ID. Ask our team for current promotional offers.

Our published prices are fixed and reflect the quality of service and products we use. However, we regularly run promotional offers and our membership plans offer excellent ongoing savings.

Membership

We offer Silver (monthly at $25), Gold (quarterly at $65) and Platinum (annual at $200) membership plans. Each tier includes increasing levels of discounts, priority booking and exclusive benefits.

Contact us via WhatsApp, email or visit us in-store to discuss and sign up for a membership plan. Our team will walk you through the benefits and help you choose the best plan for your grooming needs.

Loyalty points are earned on every appointment, referral and review you submit. Points can be redeemed for service discounts or free add-on treatments. Membership tiers earn points at a multiplied rate.
